Dryp by Evolut Visit

Athleisure e-commerce · Meta Ads + Shopify CRO

Took the brand from 0.2 to 2 ROAS in 45 days. Started by mining competitor reviews and Amazon/Flipkart listings to find real customer language and actual pain points, then rebuilt ad copy and creative around what customers already said in their own words.

When traffic still wasn't converting after the creative overhaul, the problem shifted to the storefront. Used Hotjar and heatmaps to trace where users dropped off on the Shopify store, then fixed SKU presentation, social proof placement, and trust signals. Once conversion rate improved, layered email and WhatsApp retention sequences on top.

Fixed a broken attribution system. iOS 14+ killed cookie-based tracking: campaigns were showing double-counted or missing attribution, so spend decisions were being made on bad data. Moved to GTM server-side tracking and added a persistent signup user ID cross-checked against GA to eliminate double-counting.

Got the ad accounts live when no one else could. As a founding team member, paved the way for Meta and Google ad accounts on a crypto tax platform, the kind most agencies won't touch. Worked directly with legal teams and platform specialists, handled contracts and compliance documentation. Google is now one of KoinX's most profitable channels as a direct result.

Fixed the drop-off, stage by stage. The real funnel was signup → OTP verification → wallet addition → filing and payment. Mapped where users actually dropped off between each stage and built ConvertKit email + Wati WhatsApp sequences targeting each specific gap, not a generic "complete your signup" nudge.

Global expansion (2025–26): Inherited an account with ROAS under 0.2. Took it to 1.1 in 3 months running Google as the primary channel into Canada, US, and Australia.

Honest call

Spent ~$20K on Reddit as part of the global channel mix. Landed at roughly break-even, not profitable. Recognized it wasn't the highest-leverage channel and stopped pushing spend into it rather than continuing to optimize a losing bet.

03: Fintech

BondbloX Visit

Growth Marketing Manager, full-funnel

~1 year · Regulated fintech · Ultra-HNI bond investors · Built from zero, no team

Owned growth end-to-end for a regulated fintech platform serving ultra-HNI bond investors, no team, no existing playbook, built from zero.

Lifecycle & Retention

Built CleverTap segmentation across three user states: bought bonds, signed up but never bought, bought once and stopped, and ran differentiated email, push, and WhatsApp sequences per segment. A single "you haven't done X" nudge doesn't work on an ultra-HNI investor; the message had to match where they actually were in the decision process.

Acquisition: Google Ads

Ran Google Ads across global markets, optimized for signup quality over volume. A regulated investment platform can't absorb high-volume, low-intent traffic: every unqualified lead adds compliance overhead, so the optimization target was qualified signups, not raw count.

SEO: owned solo

  • On-page: schema markup, FAQ structure for featured snippets
  • Off-page: backlinks from relevant DR domains in the finance space
  • Technical: page speed improvements, load time reduction

Email Marketing

Automation and personalized sequences building webinar funnels to move prospects toward conversion before sales ever entered the conversation. The goal was to arrive at the sales call already informed and partially convinced, shortening the cycle on a high-consideration product.

Hustle Club Visit

Currently paused

Sleep & early-rising community · 21- and 30-day structured challenges · Paid

Started as a personal fix: waking at 5am to build consistency. Grew into a paid community helping people fix sleep and become early risers through structured challenges, with a personalized, therapy-informed approach.

Growth channels, in order of what actually worked:
  • Word of mouth, the first real signal the product worked
  • Cold outreach into adjacent communities (5am clubs, running clubs, book clubs), leading with value not a pitch
  • Reddit, the breakout channel. Built to 3,000+ karma writing real sleep-hygiene content, generated 200+ signups for a free resource, converted ~10 paid sales once monetized
  • Paid Meta campaigns running alongside, contributing additional sales
